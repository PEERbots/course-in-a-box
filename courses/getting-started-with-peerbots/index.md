In this course, you'll learn about all the components of the Peerbots Platform and how to use them effectively to get the most out of your social robot.

<br>


### Training Options

Content is open source and available for anyone to use and adapt.

If you prefer an interactive and guided training for you or a group, please fill our [Peerbots' Service Request Form for trainings](https://www.peerbots.org/services-4) or reach out to [team@peerbots.org](mailto:team@peerbots.org?subject=Training%20Request).



##### Training Details

Training includes a 3 hour session where we cover all the content of the course, including our content authoring guidelines and best practices, and go over interactive exercises so you can practice your learrnings. At the end of the training, you will have created social robot content that you can use to control a connected robot.

##### What you will need:
- 2 devices
- An internet connection
- A Peerbots account (you can sign up for one for free at [app.peerbots.org](https://app.peerbots.org))
