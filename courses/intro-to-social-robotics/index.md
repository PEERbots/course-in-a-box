The Introduction to Social Robotics course is built in collaboration with [Code Grow](https://codegrow.tech) and [Peer to Peer University](https://p2pu.org). The curriculum is designed to to be used as a weekly workshop series, a one-week camp, or a self-paced course. Please [contact us](mailto:team@peerbots.org?subject=Intro%20to%20Social%20Robotics%20Request) if you would like help with the course.
