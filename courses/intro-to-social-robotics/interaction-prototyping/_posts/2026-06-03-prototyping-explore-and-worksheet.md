# 3. Explore - Interaction Prototyping

Let's put our script and storyboard into action! Set up Peerbots and practice puppeteering your robot.

## Plan

Translate your storyboard cells into distinct buttons. Decide which lines the robot says, and prepare buttons for the robot in case a person responds with any of the branching alternatives.

## Build

{% include facilitator_only.html text="
- Ensure learners can connect their control devices to the face devices.
- Walk learners through creating custom buttons using Bulk Author or Table View.
- Emphasize that buttons should be ordered and color-coded to make live control easy during a test.
" %}

Create your prototyping setup and run a trial:

1. Connect your controller dashboard to your robot face device.
2. Load your script using **Bulk Author** or add buttons in **Table View**.
3. Group buttons by conversation segment (e.g. Intro, Story, Questions).
4. Run a practice interaction where your partner interacts with the robot while you trigger the buttons from behind the scene.
