# Evaluation

## Goals 

Curriculum goals:
1. Provide hands-on experience with building social robots
2. Increase Learners' Interest, Confidence and Awareness Of technology and social robots
3. Learners recognize the distinction between automation and robotics
4. Learners recognize the interdisciplinary nature of social robotics including creative writing, storytelling and interaction design
