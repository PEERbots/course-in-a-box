# 3. Explore - Robot Body Design

It’s time to create your very own robot!  Think about some of the robots we’ve seen and talked about and what you want your robot’s job to be.  You’ll need to understand what you want your robot to do, because you only have limited resources. 

## Plan

Brainstorm some ideas for what your robot's purpose or problem to solve is, then fill out the worksheet below.

1. Robot body shell or core parts
